# Code of Conduct

EHQ is a scientific collaboration. Participants are expected to communicate
professionally, critique claims and code rather than people, disclose relevant
conflicts, respect privacy, and avoid harassment or discrimination.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, off-topic, privacy-violating,
or credential-bearing contributions and may restrict participation when needed
to protect the project and its community. Concerns may be reported privately to
`alisenol@tarsus.edu.tr`.
